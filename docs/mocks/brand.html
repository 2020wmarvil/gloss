Gloss brand candidates, round 3

Decided: H6 with 1 (the full-bleed bead in Sunset), the gradient primary button and wordmark i; see docs/BRAND.md. Kept as a record of the exploration. Variations on the glass bead (H), and palettes around Sunset. The beads now have real shading (a lit top, a shadowed underside, a hard highlight and a faint reflected light), so they read as glossy objects rather than flat dots. Click a card to choose it; the In context previews follow your picks.

1. Mark

On the dark tile at 96, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px, then flat as it would sit in the app on light and dark.

2. Color

Every option is a gradient on the bead. In the app, the primary button carries the gradient and everything else (links, badges, focus rings) uses one solid color from it, vivid on dark, deeper on light so text stays readable.

3. Wordmark

Locked: gloss, crisp sans, lowercase (Inter Display semibold when bundled).

4. In context

The window title bar, the sidebar header, the empty state, and the Windows taskbar at 24 px.
