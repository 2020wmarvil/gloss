Questions, approvals and permission modes (M15)

What M15 shipped, drawn with target.html's tokens so it can be judged and changed. Each part is numbered: reply with the number and what to change. Sections 1 to 5 are the pieces; 6 puts them in the window. Open decisions are at the end.

1Question card

An ACP form elicitation (Claude Code's AskUserQuestion, or an MCP server's form), as the last item of the log of the thread whose turn asked. The card's left edge and icon are in the waiting color. Choices are radio or checkbox cards; picked ones use the accent (it's the user's own choice). The agent's "Other" box sits inside its choice. Submit is primary, Decline secondary (the agent carries on without an answer), Cancel ghost (the agent's request is abandoned).

Decisions to make

  • D1 · Waiting color. Waiting reuses the queued amber, so "2 queued" and "Waiting for you" look alike. Keep it (both mean "not running yet"), or give waiting its own color?
  • D2 · A record of the answer. Once answered, the card disappears; only the tool row stays, and what you picked or approved isn't visible in the log (1d, 2d). Add a compact record under the tool row, as drawn in 1d?
  • D3 · Placement. The card is the last item of its thread's log, so it can scroll away while you read. Keep it in the log, or pin it above the composer until answered?
  • D4 · Why Submit is disabled. Today in its tooltip only; show it beside the buttons as drawn in 1c?
  • D5 · Keyboard. Decided and built (1a, 2a): number keys pick, Enter submits or approves once, Esc declines or rejects, while the card has focus. It takes focus when it appears unless you're typing elsewhere, and gives it back to the composer once answered.
  • D6 · Option buttons. The agent's own names and order (Claude's long "Yes, allow all edits during this session"). Keep them verbatim, or shorten "always" options to "Always" with the full text in the tooltip?
